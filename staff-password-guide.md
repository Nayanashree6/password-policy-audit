# Staff Password Security Guide

## How to Keep Your Work Accounts Safe

### 1. Use Long Passwords

Choose a long password or passphrase that is difficult to guess. For a password used as the only authentication factor, use at least 15 characters.

### 2. Use Unique Passwords

Never reuse your work password for personal accounts. Give every account its own password.

### 3. Use a Password Manager

Use an approved password manager to generate and securely store strong passwords.

### 4. Avoid Common Passwords

Do not use common passwords, predictable patterns, or easily guessed personal information.

### 5. Enable Multi-Factor Authentication

Use multi-factor authentication (MFA) to add another layer of protection to your accounts.

### 6. Never Share Passwords

Keep your passwords private. Do not share them with colleagues or send them through unsecured messages.

### 7. Change Compromised Passwords

Change your password promptly if you suspect it has been stolen or exposed.

### 8. Report Suspicious Activity

Contact your IT or security team if you notice unexpected login alerts or suspicious account activity.

## Remember

**Long passwords + unique passwords + MFA = Better account security.**
